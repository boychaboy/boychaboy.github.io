Younghun is an AI Research Engineer at NAVER Search, where he builds production LLM systems for search and commerce. His work spans retrieval-augmented generation, structured query planning, grounded generation, agentic workflows, evaluation, model distillation, and large-scale inference. He has contributed to the launches of [Cue:](https://cue.search.naver.com), [AI Briefing](https://blog.naver.com/naver_search/223807628464), and [Shopping Agent](https://mkt.naver.com/shopping_agent).

His recent research includes [Tree-of-Question](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-industry.35/), a structured retrieval framework for complex Korean question answering presented at the NAACL 2024 Industry Track. Before joining NAVER, he worked at the KAIST Users & Information Lab with Professor Alice Oh on responsible NLP and led the construction of the [Korean Offensive Language Dataset](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.744/).

He received his M.S. in Computer Science from Sogang University under the supervision of Professor Jungyun Seo. He also holds a B.A. in Chinese Culture and a B.S. in Computer Science from Sogang University. His interests include reliable LLM applications, retrieval and agent systems, human–AI interaction, and responsible AI.
