Younghun is a research engineer at NAVER Corporation, where he develops conversational agent systems. Before joining NAVER, he worked as a researcher at the KAIST U&I Lab, collaborating with Professor Alice Oh on a Korean offensive language dataset.

He received his M.S. in Computer Science under the supervision of Professor Jungyun Seo. He also holds a B.A. in Chinese Culture and a B.S. in Computer Science from Sogang University.

His research interests lie in language models, human–AI interaction, and responsible AI. He studies how human values and biases shape data creation and AI behavior, with the goal of building inclusive and trustworthy systems that effectively assist and collaborate with people.
